Workload-scaling sanity check for the star bench. star1 dispatches one worker; star3 dispatches three workers running the same executor prompt in parallel. Per-run total tokens (sum across workers) and per-run estimated cost are plotted with per-run dots and a P50 median tick. star3 / star1 ratios: tokens 2.86×, cost 2.87× — both close to the ideal 3× the topology predicts, confirming the experiment actually did 3× the LLM work and that any wall-time savings in Fig 13 reflect genuine parallelism rather than reduced load.
