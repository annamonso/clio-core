Cross-host fan-out parallelism for the star bench. star1 dispatches one worker (1 controller → 1 worker on a remote node); star3 dispatches three workers in parallel via `concurrent.futures.ThreadPoolExecutor`. For each run we measure the per-worker `claude -p` wall time and report two aggregates: the critical path (max) — what the fan-out actually costs end-to-end — and the serial baseline (sum) — what running the same workers one at a time would have taken. star1 trivially has max = sum (one worker). star3 shows max = 55.8 s vs sum = 153.4 s, a ≈2.75× speedup, close to the ideal 3× the topology permits. The shortfall vs ideal is consistent with per-node `claude -p` startup variance (each thread issues its own `srun … claude -p`).
