Distribution of per-edge ingest latency (`edge_latency_ms`) measured as the controller→leader curl RTT for a `start` POST to `/api/_inter-agent/ingest`. star1 fires one POST per run; star3 fires three concurrent POSTs from a single controller node against the leader's single-threaded Werkzeug handler. star3 includes the three edge measurements from star3-003 (whose run-level success failed at the post-run graph fetch — edge POSTs themselves succeeded). star1 P50 = 7.70 ms; star3 P50 = 9.16 ms; ratio = 1.19×. A ratio close to 1.0× indicates the leader's ingest path is fast enough that 3× concurrent POSTs do not visibly serialize at this scale. The single ~36 ms outlier in star1 is consistent with one-off scheduling jitter.
